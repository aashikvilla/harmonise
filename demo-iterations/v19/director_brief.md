# v20 direction: replace the screen tour with a visual story

## What v19 establishes

The v19 cold review scored **74/100** for film persuasion (20 first-15-second clarity, 21 human journey, 18 role value, 15 craft); fixed rubric **73/100**. It credited the family-pressure premise, correction controls, and candid prepared/no-model labels. It still experienced the film as a long text-heavy walkthrough. The audience arrived after the opening thought, the correction was easy to miss at normal speed, captions sometimes competed with the page, and the Ravi form appeared twice. The release verifier confirmed the product states are truthful and media is sound, but also counted the duplicate pre-edit and edited forms.

The 12-second kinetic-text opening was clearly fictional and introduced the promise by 4.8 seconds, but the intended audience did not appear until about 15 seconds. The film used about 93% UI footage; that share did not solve the static-scroll problem. Use fewer, more consequential UI shots and spend the remaining time on restrained kinetic storytelling with Arjun’s existing fictional words. Keep the audience explicit in the opening.

This is still a frontend-only prepared preview. Do not imply a model ran, peers are live, a moderator acted, a provider is available, or a message was sent. Keep the prepared/not-connected labels legible and adjacent. The cold reviewer judges only the exported MP4; code and tests do not count as film evidence. AAC was inaccessible to the v19 judge, so captions must carry every substantive claim.

## Film-wide direction

- Change the proportion deliberately: target **70–80% actual React footage** rather than 93%, while retaining the real correction, story, share preview, and private-choice states. Use the remaining time for restrained kinetic text built only from Arjun’s existing fictional words and the product promise; label it as a prepared product-story graphic.
- Cut on actual state changes: “Not quite,” edited wording, day transition, Ravi question edit, and “Keep privately.” Do not create motion by scrolling unchanged pages for long stretches.
- Use large, close crops of the relevant content, not a reduced whole page surrounded by whitespace. Keep a caption-safe band and maintain normal reading speed.
- Burn all substantive narration into captions. No unexplained static or narration-free hold should exceed five seconds. If AAC remains inaccessible to the cold judge, the film still needs to make sense muted.
- Preserve the fictional/prepared/no-model boundaries. Do not add actors, testimonials, generated replies, model-output mockups that could look real, or claims of a live service.
- Target **176–179 seconds**, A/V delta under **50 ms**, full decode, ordered non-overlapping captions, and a final line within two seconds of the end. Verify the opening, correction, thread, one Ravi preview, and final choice at 1920×1080 and 960×540.
- Give the v20 cold judge only the final MP4 and video-only v2.1 rubric. Treat score changes across fresh judges as directional, not proof of causation.
