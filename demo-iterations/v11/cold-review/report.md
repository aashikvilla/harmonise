3. **48–78s — Make prepared content unmistakable.** Keep a prominent “fictional prepared examples; no live posts” label over the timeline. Expected: no confusion with real community activity. Verify with a cold-viewer question.
4. **84–105s — Compress consent proof.** Show unchecked consent and disabled action, then cancel or explicit consent with exact preview. Expected: audience, alias, excluded context, and send status are clear. Verify viewers can name each.
5. **132–178s — Tighten the close.** Replace repeated holds with edit, preview, consent, and a concise ending that nothing was sent. Expected: retain agency with forward motion. Verify viewers recall the change and send status.
