## Authenticity, model attribution, accessibility, and craft

The film labels the opening scenario fictional at 00:00, posts prepared/historical around 00:40–01:00, the Ask Ravi sequence illustrative at 01:10, and drafts local with no send action connected around 01:30–02:10. Social activity is staged, not evidence of members or moderation. The sequence is linear; screen changes show prepared states but cannot establish behavior beyond the preview. Muse Spark and optional voice transcription are named as planned; the film explicitly says no model is connected. It substantiates a proposed role and user-control design, not model reasoning or calls. The professional directory is unconnected at 02:10.

Burned-in subtitles are persistent, legible, and aligned with the scenes. Spoken wording, delivery, and sound quality cannot be judged. Text is generally readable, though compact labels require attention and the centered UI leaves whitespace. Motion is restrained; correction and the optional-sharing branch are emotional highs, while static pages reduce momentum.

## Five highest-impact fix tickets

1. **00:00 — Explanatory opening.** Replace the static intro with a brief, clearly fictional moment of family pressure followed by the private-reflection promise. Verify that first-time viewers identify the problem and audience within 15 seconds.
2. **00:20–00:30 — AI role is only prepared.** Show a consented test or retain the mock label while showing the intended role. Verify that actual output and mock content are unmistakable.
3. **00:40–01:00 — Community is historical sample.** Add a labeled prototype interaction, including the possibility of no reply. Verify that no simulated participant appears live.
4. **01:10–02:10 — Consent takes several screens.** Compress audience preview, edited draft, private-context exclusion, and consent-off state into one readable sequence. Verify no sharing appears before affirmative consent.
5. **02:10–02:30 — Support ends at an unavailable directory.** Show a useful return path or locally verifiable support route while retaining the limitation. Verify no provider or appointment appears available without evidence.
