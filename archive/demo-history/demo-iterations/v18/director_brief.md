## Film-wide rules

- Use kinetic typography only for Arjun’s existing fictional words and the concise product proposition. It must be visually distinct from the app and labeled as a prepared product-story graphic; it cannot resemble an AI response or testimonial.
- Keep at least about **70% of the film** on actual React footage. Use a small number of deliberate cuts tied to a real selection or text change instead of panning over unchanged pages.
- Preserve v13’s pacing and story balance. Do not carry forward v14–v18’s extra opening cards, four-step explanatory mock-up, long provider/community tours, repeated consent screens, or near-identical end states.
- Burn all substantive claims into captions. Keep captions clear of controls and readable at 1920×1080 and 960×540. No unexplained still hold longer than five seconds.
- Target 176–179 seconds, audio/video duration difference under 50 ms, full decode, ordered non-overlapping captions, and a final spoken/captioned line within two seconds of the end.
- The cold reviewer receives only the exported MP4 and video-only rubric. Code and tests do not count toward technical depth. No score gain is assumed; v19 is a controlled test of the film language, not proof that any single edit caused a result.
