## Film-wide direction

Use actual captured React states for all interaction evidence. Make the selected words, corrected phrase, and private-only outcome the visual anchors; shorten repeated consent and provider screens. Prefer visible selections and changed text over pans across unchanged pages. A small product-explanation graphic is acceptable only when it is clearly labeled as a prepared illustration and does not resemble generated output. Keep captions burned in, high-contrast, and clear of controls; review normal playback and paused frames at 1920×1080 and 960×540. Avoid unexplained stillness longer than five seconds. Preserve v16 and its score. The v17 cold judge receives only the exported MP4 and video-only v2.1 scorecard; no code or test result may affect the grade.
