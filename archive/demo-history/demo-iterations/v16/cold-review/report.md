It says “prepared local mock-up,” “possible themes are not generated,” “no model connected,” and “nothing is sent.” Editable screens and changing preview show a staged prototype, not a real user transformation. The Day 4/9/16 thread is fictional; no live people or moderation are connected. The film says no provider is verified or bookable. These disclosures are credible, though polished thread UI may look operational at a glance.

## Meta model attribution

The correction/confirmation sequence at 0:20–0:35 makes this human-controlled role understandable. Yet the film says no model is connected and themes/replies are prepared. It substantiates a proposed role and interaction design, not execution, quality, or necessity over a non-model alternative. Peers, host, and possible moderator are also proposed/fictional roles, not live operations.

## Skeptical judge objection

This is a thoughtful, privacy-aware interface film, but AI and community value depend on prepared content. No model, live participants, moderation, or verified provider is connected. I can credit interaction design and consent controls, but cannot judge model behavior or whether the network helps people. The user ends with a private draft; nothing shows what happens outside the prototype.

## Best-case reaction

The film understands a subtle emotional problem and gives the user control over language, interpretation, and sharing. The user can correct framing, inspect the group and alias that would receive a question, and keep a useful draft private. The assistant role is modest; human perspectives are optional, not authoritative. Candid disclosures make the prototype feel trustworthy and offer a credible starting point for a future live product.

## Release recommendation

**Hold for one focused film revision, then reconsider.** Prepared examples carry much of the value; no live model, peer interaction, or verified provider is shown; the ending is a local draft, not a human outcome. Keep disclosures, sharpen the sample/proposed distinction, and replace repeated consent text with the private outcome.
