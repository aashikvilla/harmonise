# Live product film · V1

**Runtime:** about 4:57, including live response time and a closing pause. The narration is about 4:25. 1920×1080 at 25 fps. This is a real browser capture with authored narration; no synthetic product UI.

## Story promise

Harmonise turns a private, difficult moment into a possible next step, while keeping the member in control of what is shared and when. Meta helps with reflection, transcription, and navigation. Supabase holds the actual account and community records. People remain responsible for joining, sharing, community coverage, and moderation decisions.

## Recording rules

- Let every network action finish naturally. Use a quiet pause rather than a cut over a spinner.
- Scroll at a steady pace, then stop moving while the narration covers the text.
- Never show cookies, developer tools, email addresses beyond the shared demo label, member post bodies, or private identifiers.
- If the actual model label, response, voice transcript, or community counts do not appear, stop the take and fix the live issue. Do not narrate a success the screen does not prove.
- Review the whole export once at normal speed. Check visual legibility, voice intelligibility, caption accuracy, runtime, and that no production member story is visible.

## Product and technology claims to keep exact

- Meta Muse Wonder is tried first for advice/setup; Muse Spark is the fallback. The on-screen model label is the evidence for a specific answer.
- Meta Muse Voice Transcribe is separate from text generation. It transcribes speech to text; the member edits and decides whether to send it.
- Supabase Auth/Postgres back accounts and community records. Current production list contains one populated community (58 active memberships, 15 visible posts), plus two approved but empty communities.
- No measured efficacy, conversion, or safety outcome study was found. Describe intended impact, not measured results.
