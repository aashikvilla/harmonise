# Live product film · V2

**Target runtime:** 3:00. The narration is generated separately and mixed into the final H.264/AAC MP4. Captions are burned in and supplied as SRT. 1920×1080 at 25 fps. All app footage is captured from the working browser session.

## Story promise

Show why Harmonise exists before showing its screens: a difficult moment can begin privately; practical guidance and peer support are available by choice; people remain accountable for consequential decisions. The impact described is intended, not measured.

The scene lengths include live service time. If a model call takes longer than the allocated hold, shorten static home holds only. Never cut a model response in half or replace it with a prepared answer. Keep the final runtime at or below three minutes by trimming only the ending pause after the full interaction is visible.
