# Harmonise cinematic product demo — recording script

**Target cut:** 8:30, with a 6:00 core cut noted below. The full cut demonstrates the real account path, AI touchpoints, community lifecycle, moderation boundary, and adjacent prototype features without implying every feature is production-ready. Email/password signup removes the inbox wait; the shared demo account provides one-tap entry.

**Narrative:** A person under pressure wants a calm next step, then a safe way to find people who understand. Harmonise makes the step private and user-directed; AI helps reflect and organize, while people remain in control of communities and decisions.

**Presenter guardrail:** Use only synthetic identities and data. The one-tap production demo account is shared; persisted activity may be visible to other visitors using it. Model output is live and variable. Do not promise clinical outcomes, confidentiality/retention beyond verified terms, a peer reply, rapid moderation, or staffed coverage. Do not imply that `/app` prototype activity is persisted.

3. One extra synthetic pending proposal, one staged report with AI triage completed but no human action yet, one staged moderation decision/appeal example, and one pending supporter application. Never fabricate that AI made an enforcement decision.
4. Record `fixture pack version`, staging URL, deployed commit, and a reset procedure. Seed idempotently and only when the Supabase project ref is explicitly confirmed as staging. Do not reuse the broken legacy seed-user script or run any seeder against production.
5. Ensure the moderation worker is not presented as continuous coverage. In current production it is daily on Vercel Hobby and moderation triage is advisory; a reviewer/coverage operation must be genuinely available before showing an approval.

**Blockers before filming the real AI onboarding/adviser:** the consent checkbox and failure text in `/connect` say Muse Spark, while production routes adviser/setup to Wonder first with Spark fallback. Update and deploy that user-facing disclosure first. ## Additional feature cards / appendix beats

These are real or prototype surfaces that do not fit the main narrative. Use screen capture inserts only after verifying the corresponding screen is available and populated with synthetic records:

- **Alias and membership controls:** join/leave, archive/unarchive where shown, blocked-author controls, member-only access, consent text, own-post deletion.
- **Community conversation:** reply threads, reactions, reporting, user-selected feature/unfeature journey, featured journey permission and public display, scope disclosure.
- **Proposal and scope governance:** AI-drafted new community scope; human-reviewed pending launch; moderator proposal for scope stewardship based on a selected excerpt; scope updates and splits require human approval; no membership/post copying and no automatic expansion.
- **Moderation:** report queue, model triage, reviewer decisions, rationales, appeals, assignments and queue metrics. Show each as an available interface, not proof that staffed response is continuous.
- **Contribution and supporter pathway:** activity-level summary, pending volunteer/verified-supporter request, human-written decision rationale. It is not an expertise score, coach roster, or clinical credentialing system.
- **Profile/account controls:** contribution export, account deletion, notifications, and saved fictional prototype sessions exist as separate flows. Show only after staging confirmation and keep identifiers/data out of frame.
- **Fictional `/app` story:** fictional relationship scenarios, session-only community journey, milestones and check-ins, voice transcription with editable transcript. Persistent sessions, if used, belong to their own feature path; do not say that `/connect` adviser history is persisted just because `/app` has a session API.
- **Urgent support:** explain the on-screen boundary and external-resource entry only if its exact links and region assumptions have been verified. Do not stage a crisis or simulate emergency response.
